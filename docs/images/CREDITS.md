# docs/images 图片来源 | Image credits

`banner.png` 由 LimX Dynamics 用 MuJoCo 渲染合成，图中机器人模型来源如下（从左到右）：
`banner.png` was rendered and composed by LimX Dynamics in MuJoCo from these robot models (left to right):

| 机器人 Robot | 企业 Company | 模型来源 Model source | 许可 License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unitree G1 | 宇树 Unitree | [mujoco_menagerie/unitree_g1](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/main/unitree_g1) | BSD-3-Clause |
| ANYmal C | ANYbotics | [mujoco_menagerie/anybotics_anymal_c](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/main/anybotics_anymal_c) | BSD-3-Clause |
| Unitree Go2 | 宇树 Unitree | [mujoco_menagerie/unitree_go2](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/main/unitree_go2) | BSD-3-Clause |
| Oli (HU_D04) | 逐际动力 LimX Dynamics | [humanoid-description](https://github.com/limxdynamics/humanoid-description) | Apache-2.0 |
| TRON1 (WF_TRON1A) | 逐际动力 LimX Dynamics | [robot-description](https://github.com/limxdynamics/robot-description) | Apache-2.0 |

模型取自 mujoco_menagerie 提交 `b065505`。渲染时统一了材质颜色，未改动几何形状。背景柱状图为示意，不对应任何真实数据。
Models taken from mujoco_menagerie at `b065505`. Materials were recoloured to a single tone; geometry is unchanged. The bar chart in the background is decorative and shows no real data.
